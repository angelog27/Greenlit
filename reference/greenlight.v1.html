GREENLIGHT

Real directors and actors appear as game pieces. Fees, tone tags, genre tags, competitor strength ratings and all dollar amounts are the game's own invented numbers, not real pay or box-office data. Reunion and chemistry bonuses are based on films the people actually made together.

Hard mode uses a snapshot of publicly reported US release dates as of October 2026. Studios move dates often, so treat the calendar as approximate. The market is seeded from the date, so everyone playing the same day sees the same conditions, pitches and hidden luck for the same slate.
